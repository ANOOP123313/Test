MY DESIGN PROCESS

01
DISCOVER

Research users, stakeholders and the competitive landscape to build a solid foundation of insight before touching any design tool.

02
DEFINE

Synthesise research into clear problem statements, user personas and journey maps that align the team on what really needs solving.

03
IDEATE

Sketch, wireframe and explore multiple directions rapidly — embracing divergent thinking before converging on the strongest concept.

04
PROTOTYPE

Build interactive Figma prototypes at varying fidelity to communicate design intent and gather early feedback from real users.

05
TEST & DELIVER

Run usability tests, refine the experience from feedback, and deliver final design systems and specs ready for development.
